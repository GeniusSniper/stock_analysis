Auto-researcher — it picks the stocks

Press Start and the app works on its own: it screens the entire US universe (~20,000 stocks) in one sweep, then grinds down the ranked candidate list — for each stock it loads ~10 years of history, detects the market regime, backtests all six strategies, and reaches a BUY / WATCH / SKIP verdict. It automatically manages the practice portfolio with virtual money — 60% in up to 3 long-term holds, 20% in up to 2 short-term trades (fast mean-reversion setups, sold when their signal turns), and a 20% cash reserve — selling whatever drops out of its lists. It researches forever until you press Stop, and explains every decision below. Deep research is deliberately slow (~2–60 s per stock — public data feeds). Virtual money, education only — not financial advice.
