Auto-trader — it lives through the market on its own

This robot is born five years in the past with $1,000 of virtual money and lives forward one market day at a time. Its world is a merit mix — the biggest stocks PLUS the strongest recent movers, the best-rated, and the most traded — not just the largest by size. It carries a monthly goal (default $2,000): a missed month makes it trade hungrier the next (lower entry bar, bigger positions, more option budget — hard caps still apply), and its summary tells you honestly how much capital that goal really needs. The rules it must obey: it cannot look ahead — every decision uses only prices up to its current simulated day, and it may only act on its strategies' signals, never on known outcomes. It is limited to short-term trading (mean reversion, fast momentum) until the account reaches 10× its start — that unlocks long-term holding. It tries different strategies: before any buy it auditions the signaling strategy on that stock's own trailing year — did it actually beat just holding? — and it learns from every closed trade (strategies that win for it get used more). It can also spend a small capped slice on call and put options (1-month, at-the-money, priced by Black-Scholes from trailing volatility only) — calls to ride signals it has no stock slot for, puts to profit from fresh downtrends. It remembers everything: stop anytime, close the page, come back — the same life continues where it left off. When it catches up to today it reads real news headlines for its holdings (word-counting, no AI — news is banned during the rewind, that would be looking up answers), mirrors its holdings into the practice portfolio, and waits for the next market day. Virtual money, education only — not financial advice.
